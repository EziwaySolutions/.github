# Eziway Salary Packaging

Eziway is an Australia-wide salary packaging and novated leasing provider
serving the not-for-profit sector. Since 2007, Eziway has helped not-for-profit
employers offer salary packaging to their people, with support for both
employers and employees.

## What we do

- **Salary packaging:** Administration and ongoing support for employer salary
  packaging programs.
- **Novated leasing:** Support for employees exploring and arranging a novated
  lease, from vehicle selection through lease setup.

## The Eziway approach

Eziway builds its service around three commitments:

- **Personalised service** — tailored support, employee engagement, and a
  dedicated client experience team.
- **Compliance expertise** — programs supported by a compliance-focused
  framework and experienced specialists.
- **Technology** — tools and systems designed to make salary packaging easier
  for employers and employees to manage.
